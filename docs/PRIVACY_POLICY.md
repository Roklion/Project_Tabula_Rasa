# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Heathcote Advisory LLC** (“Heathcote,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with this private bookkeeping and business-record application (the “Application”).

The Application is intended for authorized use by Heathcote, affiliated businesses, and other specifically authorized users.

## 1. Information Processed by the Application

Depending on the features used, the Application may process information such as:

- transaction and bookkeeping records;
- expense amounts, categories, descriptions, payees, and dates;
- account and business information;
- uploaded receipts or supporting documents;
- business-use allocations and related explanations;
- addresses and locations entered for mileage or routing purposes;
- calculated routes, distances, or mileage information;
- user account or access information; and
- technical information generated through hosting, security, or application logs.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not reasonably necessary for the Application’s business-recordkeeping purposes.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- provide and operate the Application;
- maintain bookkeeping and business records;
- calculate distances or mileage;
- generate reports and summaries;
- store supporting documentation;
- troubleshoot and secure the Application;
- maintain backups;
- improve Application functionality; and
- comply with applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.

Information is not sold for advertising purposes.

## 3. Google Maps Platform

The Application may use Google Maps Platform services, including services used for address autocomplete, location identification, routes, and distance calculations.

When these features are used, relevant information such as an address, location, place identifier, route request, device or network information, or other request data may be transmitted to Google as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Use of Google Maps features and content is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices, including the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Google Maps Platform content is handled subject to Google's applicable storage, caching, attribution, and usage restrictions. Where permitted, the Application may retain identifiers such as Google Place IDs for recordkeeping or application functionality.

Additional terms applicable to Google Maps features are available in the [Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/).

## 4. Service Providers

The Application may use third-party providers for functions such as:

- application hosting;
- cloud infrastructure;
- file or document storage;
- authentication;
- mapping and routing;
- monitoring; and
- backup or disaster recovery.

These providers may process information only to the extent necessary to provide their respective services, subject to their own contractual terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared:

- among authorized users of the relevant business or affiliated businesses where necessary for legitimate business purposes;
- with service providers that support the Application;
- where required by law, legal process, or governmental authority;
- to investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse; or
- in connection with a business restructuring, transfer, or succession involving the Application or an affiliated business.

Heathcote does not sell personal information to third parties for advertising purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

Business and bookkeeping records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, accounting, tax, legal, backup, or recordkeeping purposes.

Retention periods may vary by record type and by the requirements applicable to the business using the Application.

Information transmitted to or received from third-party services may also be subject to those providers’ retention policies and contractual restrictions.

## 7. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect the Application and information stored through it.

However, no electronic system, network, storage platform, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their passwords, authentication credentials, devices, and access to the Application.

## 8. Access and Corrections

Authorized users may generally review or correct business information through the Application where the applicable feature permits editing.

Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of information that cannot be handled directly through the Application may be directed to Heathcote.

Certain records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, audit, backup, or legitimate business purposes even after a deletion request.

## 9. Children

The Application is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Application, applicable law, or third-party services.
